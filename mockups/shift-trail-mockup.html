Shift trail — three treatments

Demand shifts right by one step. Tails are anchored to the horizontal gridlines, so every tail moves sideways — that is what breaks the "it looks like it's going up" illusion. No large head dots. Watch the low-price rows (bottom of the grid): their intersection runs off the right edge part-way through, so those tails have to drop out. That is real, not a bug in the mockup.
