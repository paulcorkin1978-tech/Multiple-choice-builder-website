Preliminary & HSC Economics

Tariffs: weighing it up

Arguments for

  • Protects domestic producers and jobs from cheaper imports
  • Raises revenue for the government
  • Can shelter infant industries while they build scale

    • Arguments against

  • Consumers pay a higher price and buy less
  • Resources shift to less efficient domestic production (deadweight loss)
  • Invites retaliation, harming exporters
